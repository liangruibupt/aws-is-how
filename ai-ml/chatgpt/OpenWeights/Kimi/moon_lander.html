How to land (and how it is scored)
  • ← → adjust attitude (rotate the rocket). ↑ fires the main engine to climb, ↓ fires the descent thruster to drop faster. Both burn fuel.
  • The surface is rugged. Find a flat spot: touching down on a slope steeper than 10° topples the rocket.
  • A landing counts only if the rocket stays upright: tilt under 10°, descent under 16 px/s, drift under 12 px/s.
  • Touch down harder than that and it crashes; land tilted or on a slope and it tips over — both are failures.
  • Gauges turn green when a value is inside the landing limit, red when it is not. Ground slope appears below 150 px altitude.
